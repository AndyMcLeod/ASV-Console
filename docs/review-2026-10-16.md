# ASV Console: bug and reliability review, 2026-10-16

Andy: "review the ASV Console project. Check for bugs and reliability", then "generate a report on this put it
in the docs folder and then fix all in order". This is the report. The fixes landed the same day as `32c2d94`
(merged into master; the handoff commit `819918e` followed); each finding below names the files and lines as
they stood at `6f6cb0e`, before any fix. The Resolution section at the end says what each finding is now.
A Word rendering of this file sits beside it (`review-2026-10-16.docx`, made by
`node tools/review_to_docx.js docs/review-2026-10-16.md`); the Markdown is the source.

## Scope and method

The project at `6f6cb0e`: `asv_console.py` (10,866 lines), `static/asv.html` (21,513), 34 modules under
`static/js/` (18,780), eight Python side modules (`speed_zones.py`, `ais_service.py`, `stream_fusion.py`,
`ofs_s3.py`, `stofs.py`, `currents.py`, `roc_tracks.py`, `gps_sim.py`), and 148 test suites under `tests/`.

Six reviewers each took an area (the server core; the chart reader; the engine, vessel link and monitors; the
page's control logic; the path-planning modules; the Python side modules). Each was briefed from the code,
not from a summary, and told to report only a defect it could show with a failing input or state, and to
discard anything the suites already pin. Every finding below was then verified a second time by the author
of this report: run from the reviewer's probe where one existed, or traced through the code where the window
is too narrow to stage. A finding that did not survive that is not here.

Baseline: all 148 suites pass (exit 0, no FAIL line), run once sequentially before any change. Mechanical
sweeps: every Python module compiles; every JS module parses and loads under node (the three web workers
excepted, which need a browser global); pyflakes reports nothing but three `global` declarations that are
never assigned; the dependencies are the standard library; no suite can bind the operator's console port;
no state or cache file is tracked by git.

Severity words: SAFETY (the planner or the autonomy does something the operator was told it would not),
RELIABILITY (a hang, a stall, a wrong process, a lost read), WRONG BEHAVIOR (a wrong answer or advisory),
DATA (a file lost or corrupted), MINOR (narrow window or crafted input).

## Findings

### Path planning and autonomy

1. **SAFETY. A start inside a charted line's buffer gets a route across the line.** `static/js/routing.js`
   259-273 (the start snap takes the first free raster cell in row-major order, which can lie on the far
   side of the line), 522-532 (the end exemption samples at 2 m, so a zero-width line between two samples is
   never seen), 3857-3865 (the gate exempts the run from the start), 3716-3749 (smoothing tests the moved
   vertex, never the legs it makes). Reproduced: a dock drawn as a line, a start 0.8 to 1.2 m off it, a goal
   west of it; three of four geometries shipped a route crossing the line through `channelLaneRoute`.
   Charted lines are keep-outs (`static/js/keepouts.js` 904: coastline, dock lines, islet lines, hazard
   lines), and starting alongside a pier is routine. Polygons are safe; lines and zero-radius points are not.

2. **SAFETY. Routed legs can ship under the buffer.** `routing.js` 376-398: the string-pull after the raster
   search samples once per cell and rounds each sample to the grid, so when the buffer is between one and
   one and a half cells a shortcut passes about 0.9 cell from a feature. Reproduced at 2.149 m of a 3 m
   buffer. The gate (3804-3805, 3901) repairs these where the lane pipeline runs, but splices its own patch
   without re-checking it: fuzzed at a 3 m buffer over 1,500 random worlds, 12 of 446 shipped routes carried
   a leg under the buffer, 10 of them from such a patch (worst 2.88 m). A passage's pattern hops
   (`static/js/passage.js` 1135, 1148, 1199) take the planner's output with no gate at all.

3. **SAFETY, small. `legClear` reads a few percent under.** `keepouts.js` 1480-1489: the sample step is
   `max(2, buf/2)`, so a point-like closest approach between two samples passes at
   `sqrt(buf^2 - (step/2)^2)`. Reproduced: 4.845 m passes a 5 m buffer, 9.685 m a 10 m buffer, 18.885 m a
   19.5 m buffer. Every certification test in the pipeline inherits it.

4. **SAFETY. A passage's hold loses its protection from the End-of-plan RTH chain after a reload, a take-over,
   or Drop passage.** `static/asv.html` 12609-12619 (`sailOwnsHold` answers from this tab's in-memory
   `runRoute`), 12437-12442 (`passageTick` restores it only while `P.state === "running"`), 20558 (the
   chain's other gates, `rthChained` and `seqMoved`, are in-memory too). Reload the supervising tab while she
   holds at a section end, a fuel stop or the arrival and the second frame chains a Return-to-Home from a
   hold the console said she waits at; `dropPassage` (12552) nulls the record while she holds, with the same
   result; a tab that takes over mid-passage holds a stale `mission.passage` (`loadMission` runs at load and
   on a vessel switch only).

5. **SAFETY. Link restore re-greens the link but leaves her disarmed while the sim keeps flying.**
   `asv_console.py` 8527-8545 sets `armed = False` on twelve missed ticks and says "ASV failsafe: motors to
   0"; the restore branch (8406-8408) sets the link OK and touches nothing else, and the "link error" note
   stands. The state then reads `armed False, run running`; the page's guard stands down ("not under
   autonomous command") and refuses commands while the simulated boat runs her plan. Any per-tick fault
   produces this, item 10 among them.

6. **WRONG BEHAVIOR. The coarse planner's taut segments enter blocked cells.** `static/js/coarse.js`
   327-336, 379-387: a shortcut is judged on quarter-cell samples. In 300 random 24 x 24 fields, 26 taut
   segments ran through a blocked cell's interior and one through the corner between two diagonally touching
   blocked cells, which the search itself forbids; the narrow-stretch flag uses the same sampler, so the
   detail planner is not told.

7. **MINOR, hardening. NaN reads as clear water.** `blocked({e: NaN})` is false, `clearanceM` returns its
   500 m cap, `legClear` with a NaN endpoint or an undefined buffer is true through land, `guard.assess` at a
   NaN position is "clear". The wire boundary refuses non-finite values, so reachability is low; the safe
   reading of an unknown is blocked.

### Reliability

8. **RELIABILITY. A second console binds an in-use port on Windows and becomes a ghost.** `asv_console.py`
   8806-8821 and 10788: `ThreadingHTTPServer` sets `SO_REUSEADDR`, which on Windows allows a second listening
   socket on the same port. Reproduced on a scratch port: the second process reports "serving at", serves
   nothing while the first lives, takes every connection when the first closes, and a restarted console
   binds beside it and serves nothing either. `SO_EXCLUSIVEADDRUSE` refuses the second bind with error 10048.

9. **RELIABILITY. `http.client.IncompleteRead` is caught nowhere in the chart reader.** It is an
   `HTTPException`, not an `OSError` or a `ValueError`, so none of the reader's `except` clauses take it
   (2520, 2654-2666, 3134, 2400, 2537). A response body cut mid-read on any of a tile's ~600 queries
   unwinds the whole build: the child exits 1, the stale fallback is bypassed, and nothing is held, so the
   next request builds it again. The water-level readers catch it explicitly (4330).

10. **WRONG BEHAVIOR, sim. The environment override accepts Infinity and NaN.** `/api/env` (9388-9394) and
    `EnvMonitor.set_manual` (5379-5394) use `float(v)` with no finiteness check, and `json.loads` accepts
    `Infinity` and `NaN`. `math.sin(inf)` raises on every tick, the engine reads twelve raising ticks as LINK
    LOST, and a NaN position sticks until Reset. `WaterLevel.set_manual` (4921) has the same gap. Every other
    command surface refuses non-finite input in words.

11. **RELIABILITY. The speed-zone merge runs inside `/api/enc`, one query per tile per source, in series, 12 s
    timeout each.** `speed_zones.py` 249-280 and 361-395, called at `asv_console.py` 9139. A service that
    accepts the connection and never answers stalls the chart read for tiles x sources x 12 s before the
    failure memo takes effect, and again every ten minutes; the page fetches `/api/enc` with no timeout.

12. **RELIABILITY. `currents._get` can hold the currents monitor for 24 minutes.** `currents.py` 185-197:
    four tries at a 180 s timeout, no backoff, no overall deadline, called synchronously from the monitor's
    pass (`asv_console.py` 6008). A stalled THREDDS stops the current readout refreshing for the duration.
    The module is vendored, so the deadline belongs in the console.

13. **WRONG BEHAVIOR. A currents cycle whose metadata wrote but whose data did not is stuck.** `currents.py`
    484-485 writes `<cycle>_meta.json` before `<cycle>_uv.bin.gz`. After a kill between the two,
    `covering_cycle` returns that tag on every pass, `Currents(tag)` raises, the console prints "unreadable"
    and keeps no model current (`asv_console.py` 6049-6058), and nothing deletes or refetches it for as long
    as that cycle's span covers now.

14. **RELIABILITY. Chart tiles rebuilt on every request, two ways.** `asv_console.py` 3473-3492: an aged
    tile whose background re-read fails records nothing, so every request starts another child of ~600
    queries behind the served copy. 3529-3535: a tile with no copy whose coverage read fails with a service
    error (not a network error) is not held and the breaker is not set. Both hammer NOAA and occupy the one
    build slot a passage's tiles queue behind.

15. **WRONG BEHAVIOR, sim. The sim loop's `dt` is unclamped wall-clock time.** `asv_console.py` 8372-8378.
    A laptop sleep or a clock step moves her one tick's worth of hours in a single hop, through anything; a
    backward step inverts the speed clamp. `time.monotonic()` and a cap near one second close it.

### Correctness, minor

16. `Engine._publish` (7344-7351): the drop-oldest fallback catches `queue.Empty` but not `queue.Full`.
    With a full subscriber queue (a sleeping tab) and two publishers, `Full` escapes; a Stop or E-STOP that
    did reach the link answers 500 with an empty error.

17. AIVDM reassembly (`ais_service.py` 792-799) keys fragments by sequence id alone, never expires a stale
    part and never updates `total`: a lost datagram can join one ship's part 1 to another's part 2 and decode
    a report under the first MMSI with the second ship's name and destination. Display fields only.

18. The NMEA TCP source (`ais_service.py` 848-861) leaves its 15 s connect timeout on the socket, so a
    connected but quiet stream reads as "error: timed out" and reconnects every ~18 s; `roc_tracks.py`
    325-332 handles the same case correctly.

19. `zoneOnlyGovern` (`asv.html` 5254-5270): on a page that cannot say her leg, a frame with no `speed_key`
    commands nothing, yet the banner says "Her undefined is within it".

20. `speed_zones.py` 216-233: `exceeded` is computed and never read, so a tile cut at 2,000 records is
    cached for a week as complete. And its cache temp name (266, 385) is per process, not per thread, with
    no replace retry, unlike the console's `_write_json_atomic`: two concurrent reads of one cold tile
    produce a spurious "source failed" and, measured, a corrupt cache file two times in twelve (it self-heals
    on the next read).

21. POST responses (`asv_console.py` 9202) skip `finite_only`, so a crafted NaN posted to `/api/roc`,
    `/api/waterlevel` or `/api/env` comes back as a bare `NaN` the page cannot parse; the ROC one is
    persisted to `roc_config.json`.

22. A view-only tab can release an E-STOP: `SUPERVISOR_ANY` (10124) whitelists the whole estop path, both
    directions; `post_refusal` (8800) already tells a latch from a release. The page says the latch is never
    gated by design; the release is the question.

23. `Engine.state` (8603) reads `self._link` twice without the lock; `disconnect()` between the two reads
    raises. A microsecond window.

24. `RealVcu.tick` returns `{}` by design (Phase 0), so every real connect is declared LINK LOST after 3 s
    with a failsafe message about a vessel the console has never heard from (7214-7216 against 8527-8534).

### Data integrity, minor

25. `roc_tracks.py` 649 writes `roc_config.json` in place (truncate, then write); a crash mid-write leaves a
    truncated file that `_load` (603-607) reads as an empty registry with no message. The other state files
    use a temp file and a replace.

26. `_save_comms_config` (`asv_console.py` 1825-1830) is the one `os.replace` with no retry (the mission
    store measured why one is needed: 2,460 refusals in 10 s with one reader); `save_ports` (581-592)
    retries nothing either, but at least reports `saved: false`.

27. `SessionLogger._write` (997-1011) swallows every `OSError` for ever: a full drive silently ends the
    recording while the state still says it is recording.

28. The per-thread `.part` and `.build` files a killed process leaves (1617, 1794, 3342, 3368) are never
    swept.

## Checked and found sound

The mission store (locked reads and writes, retried replace, a corrupt plan quarantined and never overwritten
by an empty one); SSE broadcasting (bounded per-client queues, drop-oldest, cleanup in `finally`, the idle
tick); every monitor loop survives a raising pass; every network call found carries a timeout except item 12;
the chart cache never keeps a partial extract or tile; the clipping geometry (holes, MultiPolygons, features
on tile edges, the cross-band dedupe key); lock ordering in the tile reader; the clearance guard's rungs and
release path; the speed governor's stand-downs and latches; `cmd()`'s abort, refusal and lost-reply handling;
the route worker's stale-result discipline; the layer cache's inputs; waypoint-index bounds at the end of a
route; heading wrap and zero-length legs in the sim; the E-STOP, Stop and disarm chain; shutdown ordering;
boundary-file import validation; AIVDM bit decoding; Rule 9 handedness; the shore-rule distance index against
brute force; the passage's section joins.

## Order of repair

Items 1 and 2 first, since they are the planner shipping routes it would itself refuse; then 4 and 5, the
autonomy's state after a reload or a transient fault; then 8 and 9. Items 11, 19 and 20 are in code written
the same day as this review. Each fix lands with a check that fails on the code before it.

## Resolution (2026-10-16, the same day)

Every finding above is repaired in the working tree, in the order of repair, each with a check in the suite
that owns the behavior and that failed on the code before the fix (the narrow windows - 16, 23 - pin the shape
of the fix). What each one is now, and where it is held:

1. `routing.js`: the start snap (`freeNear`) refuses a free cell the leg from the start would cross a line,
   a polygon edge or a point to reach (`legCrossesEN`); `clearButEnds` and the gate's start exemption refuse
   a leg that crosses a feature however short its ends' clearance (`legCrosses`); smoothing tests the legs a
   moved vertex makes, not the vertex alone. gate_endpoint 13-19.
2. The string-pull certifies each shortcut with `legClear` as well as the raster; the raster dilation rounds
   up (`raster.js`); a gate patch is re-asked, never spliced past. gate_endpoint 21 (the reproduced world)
   and 22 (160 seeded worlds at 3 and 5 m, every routed leg keeping the buffer).
3. `legClear` detects at a widened radius and refines at LEG_FINE_M (0.1 m) where it detects. gate_endpoint
   20; planner_guard_seam 7f-7h hold.
4. The passage's hold is the record's: `sailOwnsHold` reads `mission.passage_hold` (saved by Drop passage
   with the run's sequence) and the passage record's holding states, within PASSAGE_HOLD_NEAR_M of the end;
   a tab that takes over re-reads the plan; the store carries `passage_hold`. passage_sail 15-15c.
5. LINK LOST stops the run (the link's own stop, run stopped, disarmed, the failsafe said) and LINK RESTORED
   says she was stopped and is disarmed - check her, then ARM; a "link error" note clears when the link is
   OK again. run_link_control 17h.
6. `coarse.js` `lineCells` is an exact grid walk (a corner pass names both side cells), so a taut segment and
   the narrow-stretch flag see every cell. passage_plan 9a, 9c.
7. A position or buffer that is not a number reads as BLOCKED, 0 m clear, named, in `blocked`, `clearanceM`,
   `blockedInfo`, `firstBlockAlong` and `legClear`; `clearanceM`'s cap may be infinite ("no cap", how the nearest
   AIS contact is found - the first cut of this guard read Infinity as unknown and the full sweep caught it in
   ais_avoid 19f). clearance_guard "UNKNOWN READS AS BLOCKED".
8. `Server` binds with SO_EXCLUSIVEADDRUSE; a second console exits 2 saying the port is in use.
   http_contract 9b.
9. `http.client.HTTPException` is caught wherever the reader catches a refusal, and `_enc_retry` tries it
   again. enc_acquire 2d.
10. `EnvMonitor.set_manual` and `WaterLevel.set_manual` refuse a non-finite number in words. finite_wire 5.
11. `speed_zones.py`: every fetch a read needs starts at once (SPEED_PARALLEL), the read waits for them
    together to SPEED_DEADLINE_S (6 s), a straggler lands in the cache behind the answer and the report says
    it is still being read; two reads of one cold tile share the fetch; a failure is memoed at the read's
    own clock. speed_zone_sources 3d-3e.
12. The currents monitor's look for a cycle runs on its own thread; the pass waits CYCLE_WAIT_S (20 s), then
    samples anyway and says the look is still out; one look at a time; a look collected after she moved
    REFETCH_KM is followed by another. `currents.TIMEOUT` itself is left as vendored. currents 6d.
13. A cycle whose data file cannot be read is set aside (its files renamed `.unreadable`), the look says so,
    and it is fetched again - at once the first time, at the next look after. currents 6e.
14. A re-read that failed holds the copy ENC_TILE_RETRY_S; a tile with no copy whose coverage read fails is
    held the same. enc_acquire 5c-5d.
15. The sim tick's dt is `time.monotonic()` and clamped to DT_MAX_S (1 s), never negative. run_link_control 18.
16. `_publish` catches `queue.Full` on the second put. run_link_control 19.
17. AIVDM parts are keyed by channel and id, expired at PART_TTL_S (10 s), the total honored, the fill bits
    the last part's. ais_sources 10-10c.
18. The NMEA TCP source sets a read timeout (TCP_READ_S, 5 s) and reads on through it; EOF ends the stream.
    ais_sources 11.
19. `zoneOnlyGovern` with no speed_key commands nothing, and the banner says the console cannot say her
    speed. speed_zones.js 8.
20. A tile cut short at the cap is served, said and not kept; the cache temp is per process and thread, its
    replace retried; the page loop is bounded by the cap itself. speed_zone_sources 3f.
21. POST responses and the first SSE frame go through `finite_only`; `_opt_float` refuses NaN and inf.
    finite_wire 6.
22. An E-STOP is taken from any tab; its release only from the supervising one (409, saying the latch
    stays and whose it is to release). estop_chain 16.
23. `Engine.state` reads `self._link` once. run_link_control 20.
24. A real link that answers the transport and sends no frame is LINK LOST after 12 ticks saying NO
    TELEMETRY, nothing commanded, no failsafe about a vessel never heard from. run_link_control 17i.
25. `roc_config.json` is written through a temp of its own and a retried replace. roc_persist 14.
26. `_save_comms_config` and `save_ports` use a temp of their own and `_replace_retrying`. cache_writes 6.
27. A session log that cannot be written says so once and stops recording. cache_writes 7.
28. `_sweep_leftovers()` at startup removes the temp files a killed process left once they are older than
    an hour (two consoles share charts/, so a live temp is kept). cache_writes 8.

Not done, on purpose: lowering `currents.TIMEOUT` (the module is vendored; the console's own wait makes the
reading independent of it - the fetch itself can still take its 24 minutes behind the pass). Still the
operator's to call.
