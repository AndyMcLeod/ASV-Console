Quick Start — Survey Planning×
  1. Plan a survey area. Click SURV, then click three points on the chart:
    1. Start corner (A)
    2. Opposite / diagonal corner (B) — sets each line's length and the area width
    3. Spacing & direction (C) — distance from A sets the line spacing; its bearing sets the line orientation
    Parallel survey lines fill the box automatically (boustrophedon). Drag A / B / C to adjust — spacing, line count and dimensions update live, or type a Spacing to force it. Click Add to plan to commit.
  2. Survey area (optional). Click BND and drop points to draw any survey-area polygon (click the first point to close; drag vertices to adjust). The pattern's lines are clipped to that area — concave shapes split legs correctly.
  3. Punch Out (optional). Click Punch Out to trim the survey lines to the same nogo zones every behaviour avoids (off land / docks, aids & hazards, water below the 1 m floor) — segments clip green, transits auto-route amber, unroutable flag red. The survey Min / Max depth is an extra coverage window on top of the nogo (survey only 3–10 m, say). Buffer and the enforce toggles are shared — changing them re-routes Go-To / RTH / search too. Composes with the survey-area boundary. CHRT / NOGO show the chart / nogo overlays. Planning aid only — verify the plan.
  4. Search pattern (alternative to a survey). Click SRCH, pick a pattern (Expanding box / Sector / Parallel), click the chart to set the datum (drag the D handle to move it), tune the params, then Add to plan. Legs clip to the nogo zones (green kept / amber routed-around / red blocked). The ASV runs it via the same Arm / Upload / Start flow.
  5. NOGO zones (automatic). On startup the console extracts ENC nogo boundaries for the operating area — shoreline, manmade features, and water shallower than 1 m corrected (charted depth + live water level). NOGO shows / hides the red overlay. Every behavior avoids them: Go-To and RTH route around them (or refuse an unreachable target), and search / survey legs clip to clear water. If there's no ENC coverage the console warns and routes direct — verify the plan.
  6. Or click WPT to place / remove individual waypoints.
  7. Set the arrival radius, speed (Low / Survey / High), and completion in the command bar — Complete (stop at the end), Loiter (station-keep at the last waypoint), or Repeat (loop the route until Stop). Applies on the next Upload; shown as the Run mode on the vessel card.
  8. Command a survey / search plan: Arm → Upload → Start. Use Pause / Stop / E-STOP as needed.
  9. Transit (no survey needed). Click TRAN, click the chart to draw a single- or multi-segment transit line, then Arm and press Follow — the ASV drives the line clear of ENC obstacles and station-keeps at the end. Go-To (single point), Hold, and RTH are the other plan-free behaviours — all available once Armed, no survey plan required.
